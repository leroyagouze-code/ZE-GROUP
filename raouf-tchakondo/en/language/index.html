Raouf Tchakondo

The language

A choreographic writing built on geometric figures, fed by the vodou dances of the South and the traditional dances of northern Togo.

The body draws figures. A circle on the floor, a triangle in space, a diamond between two points of support.

These figures are not decoration. They organise movement, trajectories, the relationships between dancers.

Each figure has a source: a dance learned, passed on, reworked.
